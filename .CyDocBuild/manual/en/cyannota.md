1. Enable CyAnnota in Settings → Plugins. Open it from the conversation toolbar or a compatible attachment.
2. Import the media/model in the embedded editor. Add the annotations, notes or views needed for your instruction.
3. Save through the CyAI integration and wait for confirmation that the archive reached the conversation.
4. Send that attachment to the agent with the requested task.

The embedded editor follows CyAI’s language and supports image, video and 3D annotations. Its optional video codec is downloaded separately in Downloads; image and 3D features do not need it. Saved files remain attached to the originating conversation even if you navigate elsewhere during saving.

```callout
{"type": "info", "title": "Keep in mind", "body": "Keep the archive for editable annotations. An ordinary exported image does not replace the annotation project. The receiving model needs a connector and tools able to access the archive and its visual information."}
```
