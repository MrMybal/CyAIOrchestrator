CyAI Orchestrator is a local Windows application. It brings provider connectors, project context, reusable agent profiles, media and execution history into one interface. An agent can use only the capabilities actually exposed by its connector and assigned tools. This guide is bundled with CyAI; reading it does not call an AI or require an Internet connection.

## Your first conversation {#help-part-1}

1. Open Settings → Connectors, configure a provider and check its connection.
2. Use ＋ in the project rail to select an existing folder and name the project.
3. Open a conversation, choose the agent and model in the composer, then describe the desired result.
4. Read the response and inspect execution details. Use the board or logs for background work.

## Find your way around {#help-part-4}

- The narrow left rail keeps projects, agents, logs, settings and this guide within reach.
- The next column contains the selected project’s conversations, spaces and sections.
- The main panel displays the selected page. The right icons toggle members and conversation details.
- The ? button or F1 opens the appropriate guide in a separate local window and preserves the workspace. Search (Ctrl+K) covers the full documentation. Use an image’s enlarge control to zoom inside the reader window. Choose English or French in the reader header; page and section IDs stay the same.

[Open connector settings](doc:connectors?app-settings=connectors)

## Read and find a guide {#reader}

Use the expandable topic tree for related chapters. Type in the search field at the top to search all pages, or press Ctrl+K for the search dialog. Hover or focus a shortened title to read its full label and description. The palette menu changes the reader theme; My space holds personal bookmarks and notes where local storage is available. Page and menu scroll positions are kept during the reader session. Enlarge images inside the window, then press Escape to reduce them.
