1. Open Methods from the agent library. Define purpose, ordered steps and supporting resources.
2. Attach a method to a profile with usage notes. Preserve revisions when changing a tested procedure.
3. Create a test with objective, inputs and expected result. Choose exact connector/model settings and launch the authorized run.
4. Inspect output and traces, record a rating with its source, then review improvement proposals before adopting them.

The editor and laboratory distinguish method revisions, test cases, individual runs and improvement proposals. Controlled loops have configured limits and stop conditions. Stop when the objective is met or another iteration is no longer justified. Personal model ratings remain separate from published benchmarks.
